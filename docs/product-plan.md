# GAA Info Guide Product Plan

## Audience

- Remote job seekers preparing for online assessments and interviews.
- Applicants preparing for technical exams and screening tests.
- Students planning international study and scholarship applications.
- Professionals learning how to work from home and earn globally.

## Core Content Pillars

- Technical assessment preparation
- Remote job exam guides
- Study abroad readiness
- Work-from-home career systems
- Global career skills and applications

## Revenue Features

- Downloadable preparation packs
- Paid templates and trackers
- Cohort-based training
- One-on-one coaching
- Sponsored guides later

## Future Data Models

- `articles`
- `article_categories`
- `products`
- `orders`
- `training_cohorts`
- `newsletter_subscribers`
- `authors`

## Suggested Integrations

- Payments: Stripe for international cards, Paystack for African markets.
- CMS: Supabase tables or Sanity.
- Email: Brevo, Mailchimp, or HubSpot.
- Analytics: PostHog or Google Analytics.
